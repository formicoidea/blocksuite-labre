Every mask of the Templates panel's Slides category is now a white slide (the white note colour, dark grey on a dark canvas): the Section, Statement and Quote masks were black. Every word of a mask now reaches WCAG AA contrast on its slide in both themes, so the secondary lines (eyebrows, details, footer, slide number) are set in ink rather than medium grey; rules and outlines keep the grey. Only a newly inserted mask changes; slides already in a document are untouched.
