The Context Map cloud now appears in the board's automatic legend ("System / Big Ball of Mud"): it is placed with a new `context-map:system` role. Relationships drawn onto a cloud stay unjudged by validation. Clouds placed before this release carry no role and stay out of the legend until replaced.
