A database view may read a source of another database (ADR 0035 amendment of 2026-10-30, red zone: stored format). A view stores `databaseId` only when its source is not in its block's database; every stored view reads as before. Hosts opt in with `DataSourcePickerService.crossDatabaseViews: true`: the view settings' "Source" and "+ view" then list every database's sources (this one's first, then the others by name, labelled with their database), and the resolver receives the database as its second argument (`ResolveHostedSource = (sourceId, databaseId?)`, a one-argument resolver keeps working). A resolver may answer `{ unavailable: 'unshared' }`: the view draws "Database not shared" (`com.labre.database.source-unshared`, `.hint`). `sourcePolicy`, `deleteView`, `importView`, view templates and the properties panel's request (new optional `databaseId`) name the view's database. "Connect a source" on one view no longer moves the block's other views under the opt-in. The disabled "Create source" line of a refused name loses its "+". `com.labre.database.source-search.placeholder` and `.create` join the core-database bundle's translation manifest.
