UML's toolbar can now be used without an editor (ADR 0045). `@formicoidea/labre-framework-uml/interchange` exports `UML_TOOLBOX`, the list of the toolbar's items that place elements (the diagram frame, the subject, the partition, the region, the combined fragment and the interaction use, every one of the 38 node kinds, and the 20 relationships as links), each with a pure builder that returns the elements the toolbar places: a classifier as its shape, its name, attributes and operations compartments and their group; a glyph as its shape, its one word and their group; a mark (an initial node, a fork…) as the shape alone; a frame with its role, kind or operator; a relationship connector in its tool's line. A host with no editor (a server, an MCP tool) writes them with `surface.addElement` or the import materializer and gets the same elements as a user. The builders are also exported from the package index as `umlNodeElements`, `umlFrameElement` and `umlRelationElement`, with the `UmlNodeTexts` and `UmlFrameOptions` types and the `UML_FRAME_SEED` table; the assembly they share with the PlantUML, XMI and draw.io readers is exported as `umlArtefactElements`, `umlFrameProps`, `umlConnectorProps` and `UML_FRAME_BOX` (with `UmlTierTexts`, `UmlArtefactPart`, `UmlFrameType`), and the body compartment's seed as `umlBodySeed`. The toolbar actions, the edge tools and the readers now share them.
