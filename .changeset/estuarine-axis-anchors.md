Stretching an Estuarine map no longer tears its axes apart: the arrowheads stay welded to the end of the axis they belong to, and the italic `e` / `t` letters keep the same gap from their axis, however wide or tall the map is pulled. A map at its normal proportions is unchanged, to the pixel.
